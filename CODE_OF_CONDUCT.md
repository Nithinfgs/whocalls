# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and keep discussions on topic.

Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not welcome here. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block users who behave that way.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their GitHub profile.

This is a short statement adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
